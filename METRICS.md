\# Metrics



\## North Star Metric

\*\*Consultations Booked\*\* – directly ties to Credex revenue.



\## Input metrics that drive North Star

1\. \*\*Audits Completed\*\* – volume of users

2\. \*\*Email Capture Rate\*\* – % who enter email

3\. \*\*High Savings Rate\*\* – % with >$500 savings (triggers consultation)



\## What I'd instrument first

\- Event tracking for each audit completion

\- Funnel: Form start → Submit → Results viewed → Email capture → Share click

\- Savings distribution histogram



\## What number triggers a pivot

If <2% of high-savings audits convert to consultation within 7 days, pivot to:

\- More aggressive consultation CTA

\- A/B test different messaging

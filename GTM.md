\# Go to Market Strategy



\## Target User

Engineering Manager or CTO at a 5-50 person startup spending $500-5000/month on AI tools.



\## Where they hang out

\- Hacker News (Show HN, Ask HN)

\- Indie Hackers Slack \& Forum

\- Reddit (r/startups, r/SaaS)

\- Twitter/X – build in public community



\## First 100 users in 30 days ($0 budget)

1\. Post on Hacker News "Show HN: Free AI Spend Auditor"

2\. Comment on relevant threads about AI tool costs

3\. DM 50 founders on Twitter with personalized audit link

4\. Share audit results in Indie Hackers Slack

5\. Submit to Product Hunt

6\. Email 20 personal contacts + ask for referrals



\## Unfair distribution channel

Credex's existing customer relationships. Offer free audits to current credit customers.



\## Week 1 traction

Target: 50 audits, 20 email captures, 5 consultations booked.

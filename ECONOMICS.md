\# Unit Economics



\## Lead value to Credex

\- Average startup AI spend: $2000/month

\- Average savings identified: 20% = $400/month

\- Credex credit margin: 15% of spend = $60/month

\- Annual value per converted lead: $720



\## Customer acquisition cost (CAC)

\- Hacker News post: $0 (1 hour)

\- Twitter DMs: $0 (3 hours)

\- Estimated paid CAC: $50-100



\## Conversion rates needed

\- Audit → Consultation: 5%

\- Consultation → Purchase: 30%

\- Overall conversion: 1.5%



For 1000 audits: 15 consultations → 5 purchases → $3,600 revenue



\## $1M ARR math

1,389 customers × $720 = $1,000,000

1,389 / 1.5% conversion = 92,600 audits/year = 254 audits/day



\## What must be true

\- 5% consultation rate achievable

\- Average startup spend is $2000+

\- Credex margin holds at 15%
